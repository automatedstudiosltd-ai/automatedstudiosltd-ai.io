Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy covers Control Room, the internal tool Automated Studios uses to upload videos to, and read statistics from, social media accounts that Automated Studios owns. Control Room is not offered to the public and only connects to our own accounts.

What data Control Room accesses

  • YouTube (through the YouTube API Services): permission to upload videos to our channels, and read-only access to our channels' videos and YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, likes, comments, subscribers).
  • TikTok: permission to publish videos to our account, and read our videos' statistics and follower count.
  • Facebook and Instagram: permission to publish Reels to our Pages and Instagram accounts, and read their insights.

Control Room does not collect personal information about viewers or any other users.

How the data is used and stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on our own computer, in a local file and database. They are used solely to publish our videos and to show us how they perform. We do not sell, share or transfer this data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google and YouTube

Control Room uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from Google security settings.

Deleting data

Revoking access in the platform's settings stops all further access. Locally stored statistics are deleted when an account is disconnected, or within 30 days of a request to the email below.
